FAQ

Before you start creating.

Are these real Paper Stish templates?＋

Yes. The artwork shown above uses the existing preview assets for templates in the Paper Stish website-template library. The live workspace above loads the actual template browser.

Can I customize a template?＋

Yes. Open a template in Paper Stish to personalize its supported text, photos and details, then preview the result.

What is Smart Edit?＋

Smart Edit is the canvas editor for working with editable elements and layers, including moving, resizing and cropping.

Can I publish and share my website?＋

Paper Stish supports publishing a personalized website with a shareable link and QR code. Publishing availability depends on your account plan.

Do I need design experience?＋

No. You can start with a template and personalize it step by step.
