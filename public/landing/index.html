FAQ

Before you start creating

Quick answers about templates, photo editing, publishing, and sharing your finished creation.

What can I create with Paper Stish?＋

Create personalized website pages, photo-led stories, love notes, and birthday or anniversary gifts for the people and moments that matter.

Can I customize the templates?＋

Yes. Start with a template, then personalize its text, photos, and visual details. Preview the result as you shape it into something that feels like you.

Can I edit my photos?＋

Use Smart Edit to crop, move, resize, and arrange photo layers, then add text or creative elements to finish the design.

Can I publish a personalized website?＋

Yes. Publish your personalized website to get a shareable link and QR code. Publishing websites currently requires Pro; you can browse templates and work on your draft before deciding to publish.

Can someone open my page with a QR code?＋

A QR code makes it simple for someone to open your published page from their phone, without needing to search for it.

Can I use Paper Stish for different occasions?＋

Absolutely. Create for birthdays, anniversaries, celebrations, thank-you notes, milestones, or a small surprise for no special reason.

Do I need design experience?＋

No. Start with a template and change the words, photos, and design details at your own pace.

Do I need an account to start?＋

You can browse templates and begin a draft first. Sign in when you're ready to publish your website; drafts you haven't published stay on your device.

Can I review my page before sharing it?＋

Yes. Preview the text, photos, and overall layout together before publishing your personalized page.
